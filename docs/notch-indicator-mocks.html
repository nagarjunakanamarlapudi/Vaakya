Vaakya · Mac · Notch Indicator

Recording status, in the notch — all states

A Dynamic Island-style pill anchored to the notch (or a draggable floating pill on non-notch Macs). Expands briefly on recording start, every 60s heartbeat, and on hover. Otherwise fully retracts. Hover any card below to play its animation.
